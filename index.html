A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
press play.

Do I need animation experience?

No prior animation experience is needed to start. Build a scene with images and text, choose an animation style, and use the preview to refine the timing.

Do I need an account to create?

You can open the Studio and start creating without signing in. An authenticated account is required to export a video and use account-backed project storage.

Can I use my own images and audio?

Yes. Upload your own visual assets, reuse them across scenes, and add audio to your project.

Which video formats can I export?

The Studio supports WebM and, in compatible browsers, MP4. Available options depend on your browser's video encoding support.

Does the Studio work on mobile?

For the full editing experience, use a desktop or laptop browser. The canvas, layer controls, and timeline are designed for a larger screen.
